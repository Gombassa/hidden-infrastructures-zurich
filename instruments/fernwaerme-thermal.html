Panning

sin(rel), fixed — production's only mode, 1.0 s time constant. Folds front and back onto the same pan value; a pipe ahead and one behind both read as centre (HRTF would resolve it, but Fernwärme uses StereoPanner deliberately — HRTF localises poorly below ~200 Hz, and this tone is 60 Hz).
